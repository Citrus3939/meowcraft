How it works

A clear commission process from first reference to final approval.

  1. Step 1

    Submit References

    Send character art, cosplay goals, deadline, and any fit or styling concerns.

  2. Step 2

    Consultation

    We review accuracy needs, construction approach, timeline, and quote direction.

  3. Step 3

    Crafting

    Your wig is cut, shaped, reinforced, and styled according to the approved plan.

  4. Step 4

    Approval

    Production photos are shared for feedback before the final finishing pass.

  5. Step 5

    Delivery

    The finished wig is packed for safer transit with care notes for wear and storage.
